# Privacy Policy for Youtilities

Last updated: 31/01/2026

Youtilities respects your privacy. This application has been designed to work with minimal data usage and maximum transparency.

## Information Collection and Use

Youtilities does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

All data created within the app (such as checklists, progress, and build plans) is stored locally on the user's device. This data never leaves your device unless you choose to export or share it manually.

## Third-Party Services

Youtilities does not use:

- Analytics services
- Tracking tools
- Advertising networks (at the time of release)
- User accounts or logins

If third-party services (such as ads) are added in the future, this privacy policy will be updated accordingly.

## Children's Information

Youtilities does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.

## Data Security

Because all data is stored locally on your device, you remain in full control of your information.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
